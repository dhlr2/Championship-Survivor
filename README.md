# ⚽ Championship Survivor

**The ultimate EFL Championship survival game.**

Pick one team every gameweek. If they win, you survive. If they draw or lose, you're on borrowed time. Two failures and you're out. Last player standing takes the glory.

---

## What is Championship Survivor?

Championship Survivor is a free-to-play prediction game built around the EFL Championship. You and your mates create a private room, pick a team each week, and hope they come through for you.

The twist? You can only pick each team **once** the entire season. As the weeks go by, your options get more limited, the pressure builds, and one by one players get knocked out — until only one remains.

---

## How to Play

### 1. Create an Account
Sign up at [championship-survivor.vercel.app](https://championship-survivor.vercel.app) with your email and a username. It's completely free.

### 2. Create or Join a Room
- **Create a room** — give it a name and share the 6-character code with your friends
- **Join a room** — enter the code you've been given to jump straight in

### 3. Wait for the Game to Start
The room creator starts the game when everyone is ready. Once started, picks open for the current Championship gameweek.

### 4. Make Your Pick
Choose one team from that weekend's Championship fixtures. Simple rules:
- You can only pick each team **once per season**
- Teams you've already used are removed from your options
- You must pick before the gameweek deadline

### 5. Results Come In
After the matches finish, results are processed automatically:

| Result | Consequence |
|--------|------------|
| ✅ Win | You're safe — move on to next week |
| 🟨 Draw or Loss | Yellow card — first warning |
| 🟥 Draw or Loss again | Red card — you're eliminated |

### 6. Survive
The game continues week after week until only one player remains. That player is the **Last Man Standing**.

---

## The Card System

Think of it like football — two strikes and you're out.

- **No cards** — you're safe and in the game
- **🟨 Yellow card** — you drew or lost once, one more chance
- **🟥 Red card** — you drew or lost twice, you're eliminated

Even after elimination you can still watch the room, see other players' picks, and follow the action — you just can't make any more picks.

---

## Strategy Tips

- **Don't waste the big clubs early** — teams like Leeds, Sunderland and Sheffield United will win most weeks. Save them for when the pressure is on.
- **Check the fixtures** — before picking, look at who each team is playing. Home form, recent results, and head-to-heads all matter.
- **Watch what others pick** — if someone else picks the same team as you and they lose, you both go down together.
- **Manage your options** — with 24 Championship teams and up to 46 gameweeks in a season, running out of teams is a real risk if you're not careful.
